Why Taiwan

A Strong Choice for International Education

Quality education. Taiwan offers respected universities across technology, engineering, business, language, design, and more.

Affordable living. Tuition and day-to-day costs can be lower than many other international study destinations.

Scholarship options. Students may qualify for government or university scholarships.

Safe and convenient. Taiwan has excellent public transport, healthcare, and modern infrastructure.
